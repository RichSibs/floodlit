Everything that matters, at a glance

Built for the moments you can't get your phone out: on the touchline, on the train, in the pub.

◆

This week's rewards

The start screen adds up what all your lineups are earning right now, and how many are in a reward spot.

7

Every card, every score

5 and 7 card lineups, captains and subs. Each card shows its live score, or its kick-off time if the player is still to play.

#

Total, rank, projection

Swipe left on a lineup for your total, your position, where you're projected to finish, and the points to your next reward.

⏱

Countdowns

How long until lineups lock, the next match with one of your players in it, and the next game week's deadline.

↻

Card by card

Tap a card to see it up close with its match and status, then turn the Digital Crown to go through the rest of the lineup.

▶

Try it first

A demo with sample lineups shows how Floodlit works before you sign in. No account needed.

Signs in the safe way

You sign in once on your iPhone, using Log in with Sorare on Sorare's own page. Floodlit never sees your Sorare password, keeps your sign-in in your devices' secure Keychain, and has no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What you need

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later, an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, and a Sorare account with Sorare Football lineups.
