Quick answers

Is Floodlit made by Sorare?

No. Floodlit is an independent app made by a Sorare manager. It works with your Sorare account but isn't made, endorsed or sponsored by Sorare.

Does it work with Sorare NBA or MLB?

Not yet. Floodlit is for Sorare Football lineups.

Can I set lineups from my watch?

No, Floodlit is for following your game week. Build and submit lineups in Sorare as usual.

Where is it available?

On the App Store in the UK and many other countries. It isn't available in EU countries at the moment.
