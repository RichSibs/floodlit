Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short: Floodlit doesn't keep, sell or share information about you. Your Sorare sign-in stays on your own iPhone and Apple Watch, your lineups come straight from Sorare to your devices, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

Who we are

Floodlit is an independent app for iPhone and Apple Watch that shows your Sorare Football lineups. It is not made by Sorare. Floodlit is run by Richard Sibley, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is the "controller" of any personal data described here. In this policy "we" means him. Contact: info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk.

What Floodlit handles, and where it goes

  • Your Sorare sign-in. When you choose Log in with Sorare, you sign in on Sorare's own web page, in a private sign-in sheet, and approve Floodlit there. Floodlit never sees your Sorare password. Sorare then gives Floodlit access tokens that let it read your lineups. These tokens are kept only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, in the secure Keychain and in Apple's encrypted link that carries your sign-in to your watch. They are sent only to Sorare and to Floodlit's sign-in service (below).
  • Your lineups, scores and rewards. Your Apple Watch fetches these directly from Sorare's servers and shows them. Card pictures are downloaded from Sorare's image servers and kept in a cache on the watch for up to 14 days so they load faster. None of this is sent to us.
  • Floodlit Pro. Apple sells Floodlit Pro and handles the payment. To know whether to unlock it, the apps ask Apple, on your device, whether your Apple Account has bought Floodlit Pro or first downloaded Floodlit when it was a paid app. Only the yes-or-no answer is kept, on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We never receive your payment details or Apple Account details.
  • Watch face complications (Floodlit Pro). So your complications have something to show, the watch keeps a short summary of your game week, such as how many lineups are earning, the rewards they're on track for and the lineup deadline. It's kept in storage that only Floodlit and its complications can read, it stays on your watch, and it's deleted when you sign out. When complications refresh in the background, the watch fetches from Sorare directly, as above.
  • Between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your iPhone passes your sign-in and whether you have Floodlit Pro to your watch, using Apple's encrypted Watch Connectivity. If Sorare ends your sign-in, your watch tells your iPhone, so it can sign you out and ask you to log in again.
  • Small settings on your watch. The watch remembers when it last received a sign-in from your iPhone, whether Floodlit Pro is unlocked, which lineup is free this game week, and a scrambled fingerprint (not the token itself) of any sign-in Sorare has turned down, so it doesn't retry it. These stay on your devices, and the fingerprint is deleted when you sign out.

Floodlit's sign-in service

Sorare requires apps like Floodlit to keep a private app key off your device. So when you sign in, and every couple of hours afterwards to keep you signed in, Floodlit talks to a small sign-in service run on Cloudflare. It swaps Sorare's one-time sign-in code, or a refresh token, for a new access token and passes it straight back to your device. The service does not store your tokens, your Sorare account details or your lineups.

To run the service, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address and basic request details (such as the time of the request). Any technical logs are used only to keep the service secure and working, and are deleted automatically within 7 days. Cloudflare acts on our behalf under its data processing terms. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when you visit, to deliver the pages and keep them secure. See GitHub's privacy statement. The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself, not from Google or anyone else.

If you email us

If you contact us, we use your email address and what you send only to reply and help you. We keep support emails for up to 2 years, then delete them, unless we need them longer to deal with a complaint or a legal claim.

Apple

Purchases and downloads, including Floodlit Pro, are handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details or your Apple Account details. If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (such as how many people use the app). These don't identify you, and we use them only to fix problems.

Sorare

Your use of Sorare is covered by Sorare's own terms and privacy policy. Floodlit only reads your lineups, scores and rewards; it can't buy, sell or trade cards or change your lineups.

Our legal reasons for using this information

  • To provide the app you use, and Floodlit Pro if you buy it (contract): signing you in through the sign-in service, showing your lineups and unlocking Pro.
  • Our legitimate interests in keeping the app, sign-in service and website secure and working, fixing problems, and answering your emails.

We don't use your information for marketing, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

Countries your information may go to

Cloudflare, GitHub and Sorare may process information outside the UK, including in the United States and the European Union. Where that happens, it is protected by the safeguards UK law requires, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard data protection contract clauses.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • No account with us. We don't build a profile of you or link your data across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Signing out and removing access

  • Tap Sign out in the Floodlit iPhone app. This removes your sign-in from your iPhone and Apple Watch, and deletes the game-week summary kept for the complications.
  • You can also remove Floodlit's access at any time in your Sorare account settings. Its tokens then stop working.
  • Deleting the app removes its cached data. Some versions of iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; signing out first removes them.

Children

Floodlit is for people who already have a Sorare account and isn't aimed at children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you and for a copy of it, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or to object to our using it. You can also ask for data you gave us in a portable format. Because Floodlit doesn't keep personal data about you, there is usually nothing to give, correct or delete (except any emails you've sent us), but you're always welcome to ask. Email info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk and we'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first at info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk. We'll acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it, and tell you the outcome without undue delay. If you're still not satisfied, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint).

Changes

If Floodlit changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
