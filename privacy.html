Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

In short: Floodlit doesn't collect, store or sell any information about you. Your Sorare sign-in stays on your own iPhone and Apple Watch, your lineups come straight from Sorare to your devices, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

Who we are

Floodlit is an independent app for iPhone and Apple Watch that shows your Sorare Football lineups. It is not made by Sorare. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Floodlit. You can contact us at info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk.

What Floodlit handles, and where it goes

  • Your Sorare sign-in. When you choose Log in with Sorare, you sign in on Sorare's own web page and approve Floodlit there. Floodlit never sees your Sorare password. Sorare then gives Floodlit access tokens that let it read your lineups. These tokens are stored only in the secure Keychain on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and are sent only to Sorare and to Floodlit's sign-in service (below).
  • Email and password sign-in (optional). If you use this instead, your password is scrambled (hashed) on your iPhone before it is sent to Sorare, and it is never saved. Your email address is sent to Sorare to sign you in and is not stored by Floodlit.
  • Your lineups, scores and rewards. Your Apple Watch fetches these directly from Sorare's servers and shows them. Card pictures are downloaded from Sorare's image servers and kept in a temporary cache on the watch so they load faster. None of this is sent to us.
  • Between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Your iPhone passes your sign-in to your watch using Apple's encrypted Watch Connectivity.

Floodlit's sign-in service

Sorare requires apps like Floodlit to keep a private app key off your device. So when you sign in, and every couple of hours afterwards to keep you signed in, Floodlit talks to a small sign-in service run on Cloudflare. It swaps Sorare's one-time sign-in code, or a refresh token, for a new access token and passes it straight back to your device. The service does not store your tokens, your Sorare account details or your lineups.

Like any website, Cloudflare processes your device's IP address and basic request details (such as the time of the request) to run the service, and may keep short-lived technical logs to keep it secure and working. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • No account with us. We don't build a profile of you or link your data across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Sorare and Apple

Your use of Sorare is covered by Sorare's own terms and privacy policy. Purchases and downloads are handled by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details.

Signing out and removing access

  • Tap Sign out in the Floodlit iPhone app. This removes your sign-in from your iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • You can also remove Floodlit's access at any time in your Sorare account settings. Its tokens then stop working.
  • Deleting the app removes its cached data. Some versions of iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; signing out first removes them.

Children

Floodlit is for people who already have a Sorare account and isn't aimed at children.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you have rights over your personal data, including to ask what is held about you. Because Floodlit doesn't keep personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to give, correct or delete, but you're welcome to ask at info@bewarethechairultra.co.uk. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If Floodlit changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
