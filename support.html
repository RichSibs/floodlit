Getting started

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later, an Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later paired to it, and a Sorare account with Sorare Football lineups.

How do I sign in?

Open Floodlit on your iPhone and tap Log in with Sorare. iOS asks whether Floodlit may use its sign-in service. Tap Continue, sign in on Sorare's page and approve Floodlit. Then tap Send to watch and open Floodlit on your Apple Watch.

Can I try it without a Sorare account?

Yes. On the watch, tap Try the demo, or on the iPhone tap Show the demo on my watch. You'll see sample lineups with made-up clubs and players. Tap the × on the watch to leave the demo.

Do I have to sign in again?

Not usually. Floodlit renews your Sorare sign-in by itself. You'll only be asked again if you sign out, remove Floodlit's access on Sorare, or haven't used it for a long time.

Using Floodlit

How do I move around the app?

The first screen is this week at a glance. Turn the Digital Crown or swipe up for each lineup. On a lineup, swipe left for its total, position, projected finish and rewards. Tap a card to see it close up, then turn the Crown to go through the rest of the lineup and the bench.

How often do scores update?

Every couple of minutes while Floodlit is open, and each time you raise your wrist to it. You can also tap Refresh at the bottom of a lineup's summary. Scores come from Sorare, so they appear as quickly as Sorare publishes them.

What do the colours and times under the cards mean?

Scores use Sorare's colours, from low (red) to high (green). A white ring means the player's match is live. A time such as 15:00, or a day such as Sat, means the player is still to play.

Which lineups does it show?

Your Sorare Football lineups for the current game week, including 5 and 7 card lineups, captains and substitutes. Before the game week starts, the first screen counts down to the lineup deadline.

Does it send notifications?

No. The Sorare app already sends notifications, and they appear on your watch. Floodlit is for checking scores at a glance.

Problems

My watch says "Sign in on your iPhone"

Open Floodlit on your iPhone. If you're signed in, tap Send to watch, keep the phone nearby, then tap Check again on the watch. If not, sign in first.

I see "Too many requests" when signing in

Sorare limits how often apps can sign in. Wait a minute and try again.

Card pictures don't appear

Pictures download over your watch's connection. Make sure your watch is near your iPhone or on Wi-Fi, then refresh. Until a picture arrives, Floodlit shows the player's initials.

"No lineups this game week"

Floodlit shows lineups you've set for the current Sorare Football game week. If you've just set one, give it a minute and tap Try again.

Account, privacy & purchases

Is Floodlit made by Sorare?

No. Floodlit is an independent app. It uses Sorare's public API with your permission, but it isn't made, endorsed or sponsored by Sorare.

Is my Sorare account safe?

With Log in with Sorare, you sign in on Sorare's own page and Floodlit never sees your password. Floodlit only reads your lineups and scores. It never buys, sells or trades cards, and never changes your lineups. Your sign-in is kept in your devices' secure Keychain. See the privacy policy.

How do I sign out or remove access?

Tap Sign out in the iPhone app; it signs your watch out too. You can also remove Floodlit's access in your Sorare account settings at any time.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
